| D-096 | 2026-09-26 | **Undo and redo of what a person does to their photos: a command journal in the engine.** The single writer records every edit of a photo (rating, flag, keywords) as a **before and after pair** (a never-rated photo goes back to *unset*), one **entry** per action however many photos it touches (`Command::Batch`, all or nothing); `Command::Undo` and `Command::Redo` apply the pair in the other direction, a new edit discards what was undone (spec §5.4), a same-value edit is not a step, a step about a photo that has gone is dropped. **In memory, per open workspace, at most 500 steps**: the persistent history of a *version* (M2, in the sidecar) is a different mechanism. | D-097 | 2026-09-26 | **Selecting several photos in the grid**, as in a file manager. Three things: the **selection** (a set of photos, kept **by identifier** in `PhotoGrid` so that a reload or a photo arriving does not lose it; a new filter clears it), the **cursor** (where the keyboard is: the `GridView`'s current index) and the **anchor** (where a range starts). Click selects only that photo; Ctrl+click toggles one; Shift+click selects the range from the anchor, Ctrl+Shift+click adds it; the arrows, Home, End, PageUp and PageDown do the same to the photo they land on (Shift extends the range, Ctrl moves the cursor and leaves the selection); Space toggles the cursor's photo, Escape clears, a drag is a rubber band (Ctrl adds). Edit: Select all (Ctrl+A, the text field's when one has the keyboard), Select none (Ctrl+Shift+A), Invert selection (Ctrl+Shift+I). The rating keys rate the whole selection as one `Command::Batch`, one step of the history (D-096), and an undo selects the photos it touched. The selection is the object flags, keywords and series actions will act on. Rating very many photos in one gesture is one large batch on the coordinator; a background batch job with progress is left to WP10. |
| D-098 | 2026-09-26 | **Flags and the keyword panel in the interface.** **Flags**: P (Picked) and X (Rejected) **toggle**: the same key on a photo that has the flag takes it off, and on a selection the flag is taken off when every photo has it, set on all otherwise (only the photos that change are in the step); U clears.
