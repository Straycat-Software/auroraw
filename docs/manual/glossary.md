# Glossary

**Auto-advance.** In the image view, moving on to the next photo after a rating, flag or colour key.

**Bracket.** A **series** of three or more frames with the same aperture, ISO and focal length and different
exposure times.

**Burst.** A series of frames taken in quick succession.

**Catalogue.** The list of your photos that Auroraw reads to show the grid: a database on your computer, rebuilt
from the workspace when needed.

**Colour label.** One of five colours (red, yellow, green, blue, purple) you can give a photo.

**Cover.** The photo a closed series is shown by: its first frame.

**Flag.** *Picked* or *rejected*; independent of the stars.

**Keyword.** A word (or a place in a tree of words) that describes a photo; the tree is the **vocabulary**.

**Original.** The photo file as it came from the camera. Auroraw never changes it.

**Resolve** (a series). Choose the frames to keep: the kept ones are picked, the others rejected.

**Series.** Photos that belong together, shown as one thumbnail with a count.

**Sidecar.** The small file in the workspace that holds what Auroraw knows about one photo (rating, flag, label,
keywords, information from the original).

**Source.** A folder whose photos are in the catalogue.

**Workspace.** The folder Auroraw writes everything you decide about your photos into: the thing you back up.
